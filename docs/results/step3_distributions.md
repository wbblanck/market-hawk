# Step 3 outcome distributions: real walk-forward results

Margin distributions are built on `elo_v1` predictions and total distributions on `ridge_v1` predictions (the best total model in docs/results/step3_walkforward.md), from `data/processed/step3_oof_predictions.parquet` joined to final scores. Each test season's distribution parameters are fit only on earlier seasons' out-of-fold rows; with prediction seasons 2015-2024 and 3 seasons of history, the scored seasons are 2018-2024 (1,942 games). 2025 is not read. Regenerate with `python -m nfl_market.distributions.evaluate` (commands at the end).
