# Sharp-book odds feeds: Pinnacle, Circa, BookMaker

Where Market Hawk can legally get the quotes behind the locked close tiers (`docs/methodology/closing_line.md`): tier 1 Pinnacle, tier 2 median of Circa and BookMaker, tier 3 all-book median. Research done 2026-11-19, read-only: no sign-ups, no emails sent, nothing paid. Section 1 covers the feeds Will already holds keys for, read from his repos `nfl_ats` and `nfl-betting-model` (read-only clones; no secret values were read, printed or copied). Section 2 covers new sources. Pages were fetched through a web scraping service, because direct fetches from this environment to `the-odds-api.com` were blocked by the session's network policy. Some fetches came from that service's cache dated 2026-11-17 or 2026-11-18; those dates are given where they apply. **Unverified** marks anything that comes only from a third-party or competitor page, or from memory, and was not confirmed on the provider's own page.

## What the close needs from a feed

- A quote at or before `kickoff - 15 min`, no more than `MAX_CLOSE_AGE` old (locked: 120 min). Live quotes or snapshots every 5 minutes or less easily meet this.
- Timestamped snapshots, or full line history, so that *we* can pick the last quote before the cutoff. A vendor's own "closing odds" field uses the vendor's definition of the close, usually the last price before start. It does **not** meet our 15-minute cutoff and must not be stored as our close (rules L1/L6).
- Both sides at the same line, so we can devig (price close) and test balance (main-line rule).
- Terms that allow storing the data and using it internally for analysis and model training.

**Precedent for the tier rule.** `nfl_ats` (`CLAUDE.md`, `src/engine/picks/final.py`) uses: Pinnacle from the pick-time capture, one retry 60 s later, then Pinnacle from OddsPapi (`MARKET_ODDSPAPI`), then from OddsLink (`MARKET_ODDSLINK`), then the all-book median flagged `MARKET_MEDIAN_FALLBACK`. `FEW_BOOKS` marks any pick with **fewer than 5 books** (`MIN_BOOKS = 5`). Our locked tiers keep that structure, insert the Circa + BookMaker median as tier 2, and use the same tier-3 minimum of **5** (confirmed by Will 2026-11-19). With ParlayAPI's ~14 books, 5 is easy to meet; with sharp-only feeds it is not.

**Reuse recommendation.** Reuse the held keys through Market Hawk provider adapters, with keys only in env or CI secrets and never in code:
- **ParlayAPI**: done. `nfl_market.integrations.sharp.parlay_api_provider()` reads `NFLMI_PARLAY_API_KEY`, else his existing `PARLAY_API_KEY`. `books=("pinnacle",)` for tier 1, `books=()` for all books (tier 3). Not yet registered with the collector.
- **OddsPapi historical** (free) for 2026 tier-1 closes: write an adapter (env `ODDSPAPI_API_KEY`) that turns each snapshot into a quote row. This needs the vendor-snapshot decision below. Watch the 250/mo quota shared with kmodel.
- **OddsLink** live as a second Pinnacle source and the only Circa source: write an adapter (env `ODDSLINK_API_KEY`). Record its `main` flag but be aware of the junk-flag case above.
- **PinnRadar**: only after a probe confirms game lines, since 100/day is shared with props CLV.
- Several sources may quote Pinnacle for the same moment. Each source keeps its own `source` label and is never mixed silently, as `nfl_ats` does with `feature_version` and flags.

## Recommendation

1. **Live, now: reuse the held keys.** Use ParlayAPI (adapter done) for Pinnacle and the retail books, with OddsLink as a second Pinnacle source plus Circa. Poll often enough near each kickoff so the last capture before `kickoff - 15 min` is fresh. ParlayAPI has had **no Pinnacle at all since 2026-11-10**, so a second Pinnacle source is needed now, not later.
2. **Historical tier 1, 2026: OddsPapi `/historical-odds`** (free, timestamped, from Jan 2026) on the held key.
3. **Historical tier 1, 2020-2025** (including the 2025 holdout): none of the held keys covers it. The cheapest verified option is **The Odds API 20K plan ($30/mo)**, with 5-minute snapshots since Sept 2022 and Pinnacle from the `eu` region. One call per kickoff slot at `kickoff - 15 min` is roughly 1,200-1,600 credits per season (**unverified** estimate), so all seasons fit in one month. The free Starter tier (no history) adds nothing over ParlayAPI. Will has to create the account.
4. **Tier 2 (Circa + BookMaker): no held or free source carries both.** OddsLink has Circa live. BookMaker needs a paid feed: SportsGameOdds Pro ($299/mo) is the only published-price feed listing both (plus Pinnacle). Before paying, confirm that it serves timestamped pre-kickoff snapshots, not only its own `closeOdds`. Otherwise, ask OpticOdds or SportsDataIO for a quote. Until then, tier 2 will be rare and most non-Pinnacle rows fall to tier 3.
5. **Apply to Pinnacle directly** (Will, api@pinnacle.com, describing a research or pregame handicapping project).
6. **No scraping.** No SBRO or AusSportsBetting data as closes, and no ASB data in this public repo at all.

## Open points for Will

- **Vendor historical snapshots as closes.** Rule L2/L6 says the close is chosen by *our* `captured_at_utc` and that snapshots are never backfilled. A vendor's historical snapshot is a capture by a third party at a known time. Do we accept it with `captured_at_utc` = vendor snapshot time and a distinct `source` label (for example `the_odds_api_historical`), reported separately from our own live captures? This needs a decision before any backfill.
- Which paid feed, if any, to buy for tier 2, and whether to buy The Odds API history for 2020-2025. Also whether to email Pinnacle.
- Decided 2026-11-19: MAX_CLOSE_AGE 120 min, tier-3 minimum 5 books, and no paid historical feed for now (CLV history starts from our own captures).
- The book's main-line flag is the first branch of our locked rule, but OddsLink flagged a junk Circa line once. Should a flagged line with hold above some bound (`nfl_ats`/kmodel drop quotes with hold > 10%) be ignored? This would be a guard, not a change of rule; it needs Will's call.
- ParlayAPI, OddsLink, OddsPapi and PinnRadar terms of use are all unverified. Will should confirm that automated polling and internal storage are allowed before Market Hawk collects from them. Market Hawk is public: stored quotes go to the DuckDB file, never the repo.
- Should exchange or prediction-market venues (Novig, Kalshi, ProphetX) count as books in the tier-3 median?
- The 2025 season is the locked holdout. Acquiring its odds is fine, but CLV evaluation on it goes through `nfl_market.holdout.read_holdout`.
- `nfl_ats` already archives vendor historical snapshots with `captured_at` = the snapshot time, and drops books whose `last_update` is after the snapshot (`market.py::only_before`). That is a precedent for the vendor-snapshot question above.
